# FUTURE_DS_02
Task 2: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
Objective:
The objective of this project is to analyze customer subscription data to understand customer churn behavior and identify the major factors influencing retention and customer loyalty. The analysis focuses on studying customer characteristics, subscription patterns, service usage, and account information to determine why customers leave and what factors contribute to long-term customer engagement.
The project addresses key business questions such as which customer groups have a higher probability of churning, what factors contribute to customer attrition, how customer tenure affects retention, which services are linked with better customer loyalty, and what strategies can help businesses reduce churn and improve customer retention.

Tool Used:
Microsoft Excel

Dataset Used:
Telco Customer Churn Dataset (Kaggle)
Dataset Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn
This dataset contains detailed customer information, including demographic attributes, subscribed services, contract details, payment methods, billing information, tenure, and churn status. It is widely used for customer analytics and provides valuable insights into customer behavior and retention challenges.

Project Deliverables:
The project delivers an Excel dashboard and analytical report covering customer churn patterns, customer lifetime trend analysis, identification of major retention drivers, visualization of factors influencing churn, and data-driven recommendations to help reduce customer loss and improve retention strategies.
